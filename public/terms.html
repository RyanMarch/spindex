Permitted Use

Spindex is a personal record catalog companion and browsing interface for physical vinyl record collections. You may use Spindex to explore your library, review album information, scan barcodes, view collection statistics, and share read-only collection views.

User Conduct

You agree not to use Spindex to violate applicable laws or abuse third-party APIs, including Discogs, LRCLIB, MusicBrainz, Wikipedia, or Wikidata.

2. Privacy & Data Storage

Local Storage on Your Device

Spindex saves your library data, personal cover choices, tracklists, and display preferences locally in your browser on your device. We do not share, sell, or monetize your collection.

Discogs Account Connection

When you connect your Discogs account, authentication uses an encrypted, secure session cookie solely to request your collection and release details from the Discogs API. Spindex does not receive or store your Discogs password.

Shared Crate Storage

If you choose to use the "Share your crate" feature, a read-only snapshot of your collection metadata is sent to and stored on Cloudflare edge storage so that others with the link can view your crate. This snapshot does not include any personal information. Outside of this voluntary sharing feature, your collection data remains local to your browser.

No Advertising or Behavioral Trackers

Spindex does not run advertising scripts, tracker pixels, or third-party behavioral analytics.

Discogs API Attribution

This application uses Discogs' API but is not affiliated with, sponsored, or endorsed by Discogs. "Discogs" is a trademark of Zink Media, LLC. Data fetched from Discogs is subject to the Discogs API Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.

External Metadata & Providers

Spindex connects to select external services to provide album context:

  • LRCLIB: Synchronized and plain song lyrics (LRCLIB docs).
  • MusicBrainz: Release metadata and album matching (MetaBrainz privacy policy).
  • Cover Art Archive: Community music album covers repository (Internet Archive terms).
  • Wikipedia, Wikidata, & Wikimedia Commons: Artist biographies, release dates, and photography (Wikimedia privacy policy).
  • Apple Music & iTunes Search: Track durations, release years, and fallback cover artwork (Apple privacy policy).
  • Deezer: Alternative album artwork (Deezer terms).

Each provider operates independently under its own terms and privacy policies. Providers may change or update their policies at any time without warning. Access to data via Spindex is not a guarantee of continued access.
