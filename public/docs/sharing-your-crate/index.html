Sharing Your Crate

Share an unlisted, read-only link to your vinyl collection.

Visitors can browse your crate without creating an account.

Creating a Share Link

Create a share link in Settings:

  1. Open Settings from the top navigation.
  2. In the Share your crate section, click Create a link.
  3. Copy your unique web address (formatted as https://spindex.ryanmarch.me/s/...).

The link is unlisted and accessible only to people who have the exact address.

Privacy and Security Safeguards

Shared snapshots exclude private collection data:

What visitors can see:

  • Album titles, artist names, and release years
  • High-resolution album cover artwork
  • Tracklists and song titles
  • Pressing formats and vinyl disc colors
  • Genre tags and liner notes

What is excluded:

  • Personal collector notes
  • Media and sleeve condition ratings
  • Purchase dates and acquisition prices
  • Account credentials, access tokens, and email addresses

The Visitor Experience

When someone opens your share link:

  • A banner indicates they are browsing a shared crate.
  • They can use the 3D flipping stack, grid view, list view, search, and gatefold inspector.
  • Viewers can switch back to their personal collection at any time.

Updating or Revoking Access

You retain full control over your shared crate:

  • Update Now: When you add new records to your collection, click Update now in Settings to publish the latest records to your existing link. Your share link URL remains unchanged.
  • Stop Sharing: Click Stop sharing at any time to immediately delete the snapshot from the server. The link will cease working for all visitors.
