Screensaver and Ambient Display

Transform your tablet or secondary display into an ambient wall of vinyl artwork.

Perfect for setting an iPad on a stand beside your turntable or bookshelf speakers, Screensaver mode renders an endless grid of album covers drifting slowly across the room while highlighting random titles from your collection.

The Ambient Cover Wall

The screensaver constructs a continuous canvas of covers drawn from your library:

  • Drifting Trajectory: The entire wall slowly drifts in a circular motion, completing a full 360-degree rotation roughly every twelve minutes.
  • Lifting Highlights: Every few seconds, an album gently lifts out of the wall, presenting its cover artwork, title, and artist name.
  • Interactive Inspection: Clicking or tapping any lifted album immediately exits the screensaver and opens that record's gatefold sleeve in the crate.

Launching Screensaver Mode

You can activate the wall in several convenient ways on tablets and computers:

  • Keyboard Shortcut: Press 4 on your keyboard from anywhere in the app.
  • Settings Menu: Open Settings and click the Screensaver row.
  • Direct URL: Navigate directly to https://spindex.ryanmarch.me/?wall to boot straight into ambient display mode. This is especially handy for home automation bookmarks or dedicated tablet stands.

Screen Wake Lock and Fullscreen

When the screensaver starts:

  • The application attempts to engage full-screen mode to remove browser toolbars and chrome.
  • It automatically requests a browser Screen Wake Lock where supported, preventing your device from dimming or sleeping during playback.
  • Pressing any key, tapping the screen, or pressing Esc smoothly returns you to the standard crate view.
