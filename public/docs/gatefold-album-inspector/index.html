The Gatefold Album Inspector

Pulling a record from the crate opens the gatefold inspector: a two-column workspace detailing every facet of the release.

The left column showcases the physical jacket, back cover, and a realistic vinyl disc rendering matched to your pressing. The right column compiles tracklists, song lyrics, liner notes, musician credits, streaming links, and your personal collection copy details.

Flipping the Sleeve

When rear cover art is available from archive sources, a flip button appears beneath the album jacket.

Clicking the sleeve or the Flip to back cover button triggers a 3D rotation, letting you read back cover artwork, track notes, and barcode details just as you would when holding the physical jacket.

Realistic Vinyl Disc Finishes

Peeking from the right edge of the jacket is an interactive vinyl disc. Spindex parses the format description strings in your Discogs pressing data to render accurate disc finishes:

  • Colored Vinyl: Vibrant solid hues such as red, blue, green, yellow, white, and orange.
  • Translucent & Clear: Semi-transparent discs with visible light transmission.
  • Splatter & Marble: Swirled and speckled patterns matching special edition pressings.
  • Picture Discs: Discs displaying full-bleed album artwork across the vinyl face.
  • Classic Black: Authentic vinyl sheen with grooved light reflections.

The center label also displays miniature artwork and a realistic spindle cutout.

Tracklists and Synced Lyrics

The tracklist details side and position markers (such as A1, A2, B1) and song durations.

Clicking the lyrics icon next to any song opens a slide-over lyric sheet. Powered by LRCLIB, Spindex displays synchronized or plain-text lyrics, lets you step between tracks without leaving the sheet, and offers a quick shortcut to search on Genius if extended annotations are desired.

Liner Notes, Credits, and Artist Bios

Spindex automatically enriches your collection by looking up historical context from Wikipedia and Wikidata:

  • Liner Notes: Read recording stories, critical reception, and the cultural background of the album.
  • Musician Credits: Detailed breakdowns of producers, mixing engineers, vocalists, and session musicians.
  • Artist Biography: Portrait imagery, origins, active years, and biographical notes for the performing artist.
  • Elsewhere in Your Crate: Quick links to other albums in your collection that feature the same artist or collaborators.

Your Personal Copy

At the bottom of the right panel, Spindex presents your personal collector copy information:

  • Condition Grades: Goldmine standard condition ratings for both media and sleeve (such as Mint, Near Mint, Very Good Plus).
  • Date Added: When the record was cataloged into your collection.
  • Private Notes: Custom collector notes imported directly from your Discogs account.
  • Artwork Fixer: If an automated cover art match does not meet your preference, click the artwork tool to pick alternative high-resolution images.
