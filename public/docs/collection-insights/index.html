Collection Stats: By the Numbers

The statistics panel summarizes your collection by era, genre, size, and speed.

Statistics are calculated locally from your synced records.

Viewing Your Statistics

You can open the collection analytics panel at any time:

  • Click the bar chart icon in the top header utility pill.
  • Or open Settings and click the Collection stats row.

The drawer slides in from the side without disrupting your current position in the crate.

Decades and Release Eras

Releases are grouped into ten-year intervals, showing a bar chart of your collection across decades.

Genres, Formats, and Speeds

The statistics drawer also calculates breakdowns for:

  • Top Genres: Ranked list of musical categories showing both total record counts and percentage shares of your library.
  • Vinyl Sizes: The balance between full-length 12" albums, 7" 45-RPM singles, and 10" EPs.
  • Rotation Speeds: Proportions of 33 ⅓ RPM and 45 RPM pressings.

All calculations occur locally in IndexedDB without uploading data to external servers.
