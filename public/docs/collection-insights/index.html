Collection Statistics: By the Numbers

Understand the makeup of your music library with instant, visual collection statistics.

The "By the numbers" panel analyzes your synced records locally to break down your collection across release eras, primary genres, vinyl sizes, and playback speeds.

Viewing Your Statistics

You can open the collection analytics panel at any time:

  • Click the bar chart icon in the top header utility pill.
  • Or open Settings and click the Collection stats row.

The drawer slides in from the side without disrupting your current position in the crate.

Decades and Release Eras

Spindex groups releases into distinct ten-year spans, displaying a visual bar chart that reveals which eras dominate your shelves.

Whether your collection peaks in the golden era of 1970s rock, 1990s hip hop, or 2020s modern reissues, the decade chart highlights your personal musical listening habits at a glance.

Genres, Formats, and Speeds

The statistics drawer also calculates breakdowns for:

  • Top Genres: Ranked list of musical categories showing both total record counts and percentage shares of your library.
  • Vinyl Sizes: The balance between full-length 12" albums, 7" 45-RPM singles, and 10" EPs.
  • Rotation Speeds: Proportions of standard 33 ⅓ RPM records compared to audiophile 45 RPM pressings.

All calculations occur in real time inside your browser database without uploading your data to external servers.
