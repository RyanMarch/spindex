01 The crate

See the whole crate and the next record a flip away.

Spindex shows high-resolution artwork of your current record and keeps the rest a flick away. Scroll, tap the arrow keys, or swipe your way through the stack.

Search by title, artist or even a song. Filter by genre, jump by letter or decade, or hit Surprise Me when you can't decide. Behind it all, a soft glow picks up color from whichever cover you're holding.

02 Gatefold inspector

Open the sleeve. Read the whole record.

Pick any record and the jacket opens. Liner notes, label information, pressings, credits, and tracklists all displayed like you're holding the record in your lap.

Wax
See the color of your specific pressing: black, splatter, marble, translucent or picture disc.
Sleeve
Flip the jacket over for the back cover, then read the tracklist, liner notes, and credits.
Words
Open a lyric sheet beside the record and sing along with the songs as they play on your turntable.
Crew
Spot the producers, writers, and labels that turn up again and again across your shelves.

03 Screensaver

Leave it on beside the turntable.

Sit back and watch as your album covers drift slowly across the screen. See highlights from your collection with artist, title, and year.

  • Mirror it to a TV or prop up a display next to the speakers.
  • It goes full screen and keeps the display awake.
  • Press 4 anywhere in Spindex, or bookmark /?wall to start straight into it.

04 The fine print

Your collection stays yours. Here's the tracklist.

  1. Side A · Handy
  2. A1

    Scan a barcode, add a record

    Point your camera at the barcode on a jacket to find the exact pressing, then add it to your collection. No camera? Upload a photo or type the number.

    Guide
  3. A2

    Works offline

    Once your collection has loaded, the covers, tracklists and details are right there on your device. Bookmark it and dig anywhere, even without a signal.

    Guide
  4. A3

    Share a link, keep the details

    Send friends an unlisted, read-only link. Prices, condition grades and your notes never leave your device, and you can switch the link off whenever you like.

    Guide
  5. Side B · Private
  6. B1

    Your records live on your device

    Your collection is visible only to you, and those who you choose to share a link with.

    Privacy
  7. B2

    Sign in with Discogs

    Use your Discogs account to sign in. Spindex won't ever see your password or any other data.

    Guide
  8. B3

    No ads, no trackers

    No advertising scripts, no tracking pixels, no analytics watching what you browse. Nothing about your collection is sold.

    Privacy

05 By the numbers

See the shape of your shelf. Understand your collection.

Anyone can count their records. Spindex works out the things you'd never add up yourself.

  • How much does the whole pile weigh?
  • What is it worth right now?
  • Which day do you add records most?
  • How long would it take to play it all?
  • What color are the records in your crate?
